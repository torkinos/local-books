# Local Books v0.1.0

Private income books for people paid in stablecoins on Solana. Local-first, watch-only,
no backend. Android.

**Install:** download `app-release.apk` below, open it on the phone, allow the install
from this source when Android asks. No account, no sign-in.

**Watch it work:** [the whole loop, on one phone](TODO_VIDEO_URL) — invoice, share the
PDF, pay it, watch it match, export the CSV.

## What it does

- Watches the Solana address(es) you paste and pulls their payment history from public
  RPC. Progress is shown as counts; nothing is estimated.
- Creates invoices (client, line items, USDC or USDT, due date) and shares them as a PDF
  with a Solana Pay QR code. A client who scans the QR pays the exact amount, and the
  app matches the payment to the invoice by itself the next time it checks.
- Values every stablecoin receipt in GEL at the National Bank of Georgia's official rate
  for the receipt date, and shows monthly income per client.
- Exports the income statement as CSV with the rate, rate source and rate date on every
  row, so an accountant can check it.
- Stores everything in an encrypted database on the phone. The app's only network
  traffic is Solana RPC and the NBG rate endpoint. No telemetry of any kind.

## Known limitations, stated plainly

- **Reference matching only.** A client who ignores the QR and sends a plain transfer
  still shows up in the ledger, but the invoice stays open until you decide. Heuristic
  matching is post-grant work.
- **Checks when you open the app, and while it is open.** There is no background sync;
  a payment that lands while the app is closed appears on the next open.
- **USDC and USDT income only, valued in GEL.** SOL and other tokens show in the ledger
  but are not valued and cannot be invoiced.
- **Public RPC only, in this build.** The app reads the chain through
  `api.mainnet-beta.solana.com`, which is rate-limited: the first backfill of a busy
  wallet can take an hour, once, with progress shown. A personal RPC endpoint (for
  example a free Helius key) makes it minutes, but needs a rebuild
  (`EXPO_PUBLIC_RPC_URL`); there is no settings screen yet.
- **One phone.** The books are encrypted with a key that lives in this phone's
  keystore. A backup restored to another phone cannot open them; the app says so
  instead of guessing. Keep the exported CSV as your portable record.
- **Android only.** iOS is post-grant.
- **No editing or deleting invoices**, and no partial-payment tracking: confirming an
  underpayment marks the invoice paid.

## Under the hood

Pure-TypeScript domain core with zero runtime dependencies (ingestion, matching,
valuation, reports), an Expo shell around it, SQLCipher storage, 300+ tests including
real mainnet and devnet transactions. Design decisions are recorded in
[DECISIONS.md](https://github.com/torkinos/local-books/blob/master/DECISIONS.md).

## Verify the download

The APK is signed with the Local Books release key. `apksigner verify --print-certs
app-release.apk` shows the certificate; the same certificate will sign every future
release, so updates install in place.
